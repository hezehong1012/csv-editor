Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

How your data is handled

Editing, sorting, conversion and export all run inside your browser. Any CSV / Excel file you open is never uploaded to our server.

The one exception is the AI editing feature: when you use it, your column names and the first 30 sample rows are sent through our server to an external LLM (large language model) API. The full file is never transmitted, and if you do not use AI editing, no data leaves your browser at all. For confidential files, skip AI editing and use the in-browser tools only.

What we collect

The site has no accounts and saves no files. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics such as page views, solely to improve the site. You can opt out via your browser's cookie or ad settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out via the contact form.
